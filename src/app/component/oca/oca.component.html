Subordinate Help Info:
  1. Grey band/background on a frequency indicates either of the following:
    1. The submission window is not open.
    2. Your OCA Supervisor has reviewed your feedback.
    3. No action required from your end. Although, details can be viewed by clicking on the frequency band.
  2. White band/background on a frequency indicates the following:
    1. The submission window for this frequency is open.
    2. Your OCA Supervisor hasn’t yet reviewed your feedback.
    3. Action is pending from your end.
  3. Blue box + white band for a frequency means action required from your end.
  4. Date Range/Month/Year etc., mentioned besides the frequency, indicates the period for which feedback is being submitted by you. Date Range if not displayed, indicates the submission window is not open.
  5. Feedback box with Grey band shows your Supervisor’s feedback against the respective controls, in non-editable mode.
  6. Click on Attachment icon to attach a file against the respective controls. For deletion, click on Delete icon.
Supervisor Help Info:
  1. Grey band/background on a frequency indicates the following:
    1. The submission window is not open.
    2. You have reviewed all your team’s feedback for the respective frequency.
    3. No action required from your end. Although, details can be viewed by clicking on the frequency band.
  2. White band/background on a frequency indicates the following:
    1. The submission window for this frequency is open.
    2. Action is either pending or available for modification.
  3. Blue box + white band for a frequency means action required or available for modification.
  4. Date Range/Month/Year etc., mentioned besides the frequency, indicates the period for which feedback is being submitted by you. Date Range if not displayed, indicates the submission window is not open.
  5. Feedback box with Grey band shows your team member’s feedback against the respective controls, in non-editable mode.
  6. Default selected feedback would be same as that of your team member’s feedback for the respective control.
    7. E.g. If team member has selected as YES, you will see YES as selected by default which can be modified by you on this screen.
  7. Click on Attachment icon (if available) to view the attached file.
